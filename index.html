YOUR NEXT ACTION · 40–55 MINUTES

  1. Read for 10 minutes. Open the retrieval practice guide below and choose its poster or short introduction.
  2. Close it and write for 15–20 minutes. Without AI, write 150–200 words on the difference between receiving an explanation and demonstrating understanding. Use the What I learned box below.
  3. Choose these related concepts in 10–15 minutes. Briefly look at their headings and introductions. Save deeper study for Day 03.
    • What is an AI agent?
    • What is a tool, and why might an agent use one?
    • The Thought–Action–Observation cycle
  4. Record your starting point in 5–10 minutes. Rate each concept using 0 for new, 1 for familiar but hard to explain, or 2 for able to explain with an example. Save the ratings and a short note in the Day 01 box below.

Stop after the reflection and three ratings. Day 02 prepares the question bank, study assignments and README.
